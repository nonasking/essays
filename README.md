Every essay was written with heavy use of AI, and each one discloses how at its end. Most were written out of the records of conversations with an AI, through dialectical engagement between the author and the machine, typed with the AI's borrowed hands. In those, the ideas are argued for, not extracted; the process is part of the point. The Branches of *Geureohada* is the exception: the AI found and wrote down what the author asked, and the two refined it together.
